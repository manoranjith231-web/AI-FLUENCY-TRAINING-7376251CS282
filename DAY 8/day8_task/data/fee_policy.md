# College Fee Policy

## Course fees
CS101 has a course fee of Rs. 12,000.
AI202 has a course fee of Rs. 18,000.
DS303 fee information must be confirmed with the accounts office.

## Late payment
The late fee is Rs. 100 per day, capped at a maximum of Rs. 2,000.
For example, a five-day delay results in a late fee of Rs. 500.
